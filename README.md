# DealMate AI — Sales Memory Agent

> **AI-powered persistent memory for sales teams**

DealMate AI is an AI-powered Sales Memory Agent that helps sales teams remember and retrieve important customer and deal context from conversations.

Sales information is often scattered across meetings, calls, emails, and chats. DealMate AI organizes this context into structured memories such as requirements, pain points, objections, pricing discussions, competitors, decisions, and follow-up commitments.

Instead of forcing sales representatives to search through old conversations, DealMate AI makes important context available when they need it.

---

## Problem Statement

Sales teams handle information from many customer meetings, calls, emails, and chats.

Important details such as:

* Customer requirements
* Pain points
* Objections
* Competitors
* Pricing discussions
* Decisions
* Follow-up commitments

can easily be forgotten or scattered across different sources.

Because of this, sales representatives may enter a meeting without complete context and waste valuable time searching old conversations.

---

## Our Solution

**DealMate AI creates persistent memory for every customer and deal.**

The system takes conversation data and extracts meaningful sales signals. These signals are organized into searchable memory categories and connected back to the original conversation.

A salesperson can then ask questions such as:

* "Prepare me for my next meeting"
* "Why did this deal stall?"
* "What did the customer object to?"
* "What should I follow up on?"

DealMate AI retrieves relevant context and presents it in a simple sales-focused interface.

### USP

> **Normal CRM stores information. DealMate AI understands, remembers, and retrieves important context when the salesperson needs it.**

## Key Features

### AI Memory

Automatically identifies important information from customer conversations and organizes it into:

* Requirements
* Pricing
* Objections
* Follow-ups
* Competitors
* Decisions

### Ask AI

Sales representatives can ask natural-language questions about their customer and deal history.

### Customer Memory

Each customer has a connected memory trail containing their previous conversation context.

### Deal Context

Deals are connected with customer information, memory, next actions, and potential risks.

### Conversation Management

Sales representatives can add customer conversations and preserve them as reusable context.
